# R3.5 Anchor Attestation — FAM-SSCAR-FI-001 (prolog)

Written after R3.1 reading (all 6 packs' implementation-bearing normative core + the 3 SSCAR connector annexes), before R4/R5.

## Q1. What the Family Pack promises as delivered, and what the family verdict is

The 6 packs deliver a composition surface: five component agents (VRA, BDA, TAA, ARA, FPA), each a standalone fail-closed normative control with its own closed state machine, validator catalogue, controlled verdict vocabulary and offline-reconstructible evidence contract, PLUS the SSCAR F4 family integrator that composes the components' public output fields into a family-level decision. The family verdict is the EMERGENT `session_replay_admissibility` decision with exactly six canonical values (`REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE`, `REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE_CONSTRAINED`, `REPLAY_INCOMPLETE`, `NONCOMPLIANT_HOLD`, `CONTAMINATION_FAIL_CLOSED`, `INCIDENT_ONLY`), per `SSCAR_Specification.md` §4.1. It is NOT an aggregation, average, majority-vote, or first-failing-wins of the five component verdicts: the components produce DATA FIELDS, the twelve family validators FV-01..FV-12 consume them, and the verdict emerges only after cross-domain closure per §10 gate logic (`SSCAR_Specification.md` §1: "No single subordinate control decides family-level replay admissibility on its own"). AMB-01 forbids replacing the family verdict with an isolated local result.

## Q2. What the buyer-contract explicitly delegates to deployment

The SSCAR README ("What your team must produce") delegates six integrator deployment artifacts I do NOT implement: the conforming executable integrator; component wiring/adapters built via Annex A producer-consumer wiring and Annex B token identity; boundary/replay/readmission/output projections (FIC-01..03, FOC-01..05); an evidence assembly and export pipeline producing FEV-01..FEV-15 including BoundaryEventEvidenceReport, report_signature and export_manifest_ref; an operator-declared implementation profile (cryptographic suite, storage backend, serialization, operator workflow, optional assurance/attestation controls, any downstream rollback action); and a conformance harness. Each component pack additionally delegates its own companion artifacts (e.g. VRA's five: stable-snapshot facility, anti-rollback mechanism, local policy bundle, single-writer persistence control plane, readmission authority workflow; FPA's eight; BDA's ten; TAA's five; ARA's seven). These are companion deployment obligations, not pack contents.

## Q3. How the seven family test suites partition

Seven buyer-runnable suites plus one emergent observable. The five component PT suites: VRA `PT-*` (29), BDA `PTS-BDA-*` (36), TAA `TAA-PT-*` (27) with its `VP-GEN-*` sub-suite (27), ARA `ARA-T*` (27), FPA `FPA-T-*`/`FPA-D-*` (41) — each per its own pack's README/Minimal Run Note. The SSCAR integrator Public Test Suite `PTS-*` (28 records: 19 runnable / 2 parameterized / 7 declarative; pinned at 30 lettered ids). The Annex C Family Connector Test Suite `CT-01..CT-14` (14). PLUS the emergent family-verdict observable distilled from the SSCAR suites under composition (not a separate test set). Family conformance = convergence on ALL seven suites AND a convergent emergent family verdict; failure at any suite is a family conformance failure.

## Q4. Boundary between spec-derivable and deployment-substrate PTs

Records that depend on deployment-substrate values the pack does not provide are the parameterized and declarative ones: the FV-04 local-reclassification policy hook (SSCAR `PTS-007`, parameterized) and the verdict-emission-layer correctness checks for the `REPLAY_INCOMPLETE` / `INCIDENT_ONLY` / `REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE_CONSTRAINED` differentiation (declarative, SSCAR `PTS-005`/`015`/`019`/`020`/`027`; component declarative obligations FPA-D-01..06, TAA-PT-020-DECL, BDA bounded-external entries, ARA V-10/gate obligations). The boundary runs both ways: where a pack surface itself fixes a determination as deterministic and not operator-decided — Annex A Row 12's re-entry envelope (the altered-field set determines the SR re-entry target; operator discretion is not admitted) and Annex A Row 4's explicit-reclassification requirement (reclassification must be explicit, never implicit) — that determination is spec-derivable, not deployment-substrate, even while the reclassification policy details and the re-entry orchestration remain deployment-side.
