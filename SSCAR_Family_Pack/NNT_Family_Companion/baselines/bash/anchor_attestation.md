# R3.5 Anchor Attestation — FAM-SSCAR-FI-001 (bash paradigm)

Written after R3.1 integral reading of all 68 buyer-facing files, before R4/R5. Observational language per R10; spec terms in backticks.

## Q1. What does the Family Pack promise as delivered, and what is the family verdict?

The six packs together deliver one composition surface: five component agents (VRA, BDA, TAA, ARA, FPA), each exposing the public output fields that the integrator consumes, in composition with the SSCAR F4 integrator that wires those fields through its twelve family validators `FV-01..FV-12` (`SSCAR_Specification.md` §7). The family verdict is the EMERGENT `session_replay_admissibility` decision with exactly six canonical values — `REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE`, `REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE_CONSTRAINED`, `REPLAY_INCOMPLETE`, `NONCOMPLIANT_HOLD`, `CONTAMINATION_FAIL_CLOSED`, `INCIDENT_ONLY` (`SSCAR_Specification.md` §4.1) — and it is NOT an aggregation, average, majority-vote, or first-failing-wins of the five component verdicts. The components produce DATA FIELDS; the family validators consume them; the verdict emerges only after cross-domain closure per §10 gate logic (`SSCAR_Specification.md` §1). `SSCAR_Ambiguity_Register.md` AMB-01 forbids replacing the family verdict with an isolated local result.

## Q2. What does the buyer-contract explicitly delegate to deployment?

Per `SSCAR_README.md` §"What your team must produce", the buyer builds six integrator-side companion artifacts that are NOT in the pack and that I do not implement: a conforming executable integrator; component wiring and adapters using Annex A and Annex B; boundary/replay/readmission/output projections for FIC-01..03 and FOC-01..05; an evidence assembly and export pipeline for FEV-01..15; an operator-declared implementation profile (cryptographic suite, storage backend, serialization, operator workflow, optional assurance/attestation); and a conformance harness. Each component README additionally delegates its own deployment substrate — for example VRA's stable snapshot facility, anti-rollback mechanism, local policy bundle, single-writer control plane, and readmission authority; FPA's monotonic watermark store and append-only IRR journal; BDA's schema catalogue, policy registry, approval channel, and evidence ledger; TAA's operator policy profile and evidence archive; ARA's policy profile and trust store — all deployment-owned, not pack-resident.

## Q3. How do the seven family test suites partition?

The Family Pack ships seven buyer-runnable suites plus one emergent observable. The five component PT suites are VRA `PT-*`, BDA `PTS-BDA-*`, TAA `TAA-PT-*` (with the paired `VP-GEN-*` sub-suite), ARA `ARA-T*`, and FPA `FPA-T-*`/`FPA-D-*`, each per its own pack's README and Minimal Run Note. The sixth is the SSCAR integrator Public Test Suite `PTS-*` (28 records: 19 runnable, 2 parameterized, 7 declarative per `SSCAR_README.md`). The seventh is the Annex C Family Connector Test Suite `CT-01..CT-14` (`SSCAR_Family_Connector_Tests.md`). Beyond the seven suites there is the emergent family-verdict observable, distilled from the SSCAR suites under composition rather than shipped as a separate test set. Family conformance requires convergence on ALL seven suites plus a convergent emergent family verdict.

## Q4. Where is the boundary between spec-derivable and deployment-substrate PTs?

Records that depend on deployment-substrate values the pack does not provide are marked parameterized or declarative and are satisfied at the layer the owning pack names: for example the FV-04 local-reclassification policy hook (`PTS-007`, parameterized — `SSCAR_Conformance_Tests.md` §8 Minimal Run Note item 2) and the verdict-emission-layer correctness for `REPLAY_INCOMPLETE`/`INCIDENT_ONLY`/`REPLAY_ADMISSIBLE_CONSTRAINED` differentiation (declarative — §8 Minimal Run Note item 1). The boundary runs both ways: where a pack surface itself fixes a determination as deterministic and not operator-decided, that determination is spec-derivable even though its deployment-side orchestration is delegated. Annex A Row 12 fixes the re-entry envelope — the altered-field set alone determines whether readmission re-enters boundary-screened or rehydration-closed, and operator discretion is not admitted (`SSCAR_Producer_Consumer_Manifest.md` §3) — and Annex A Row 4 fixes that reclassification of an explanatory-only payload must be explicit, never implicit, even while the reclassification policy details remain deployment-side.
